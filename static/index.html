What this prototype assumes

01

Conditional forecasts

Demand is predicted from supplied weather. The sample uses historical weather; future forecasts require future hourly weather inputs.

02

Simulated storage

Capacity, reserve, inverter rating and efficiency are scenario inputs. Solar output scales with irradiance, capped at its rated peak. No measured SOC is available.

03

Honest cloud status

QuickML is selected on the server. A failed cloud request returns an error. It never silently falls back to local or dummy predictions.
